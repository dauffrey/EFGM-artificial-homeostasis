# AH-EXP-0006 — Stochastic multi-seed replication

## Purpose

Test whether the Phase-1 coupled-margin effect survives stochastic variation rather than only deterministic trajectories.

## Frozen mechanism

The regulator implementation, coefficients, mode thresholds, queue state transitions, action semantics, viability definition, completion target, and Phase-1 scoring rule are frozen from AH-EXP-0005. No regulator retuning is permitted after outcomes are observed.

## Stochastic intervention

AH-EXP-0006 reuses the four AH-EXP-0005 disturbance families as nominal means but adds bounded stochastic perturbation to each disturbance step.

For every nominal disturbance `d`, the realized disturbance is:

`clip(d + U(-0.12, +0.12), 0.0, 1.0)`

where `U` is generated by Python's deterministic `random.Random(seed)` pseudorandom generator.

## Frozen seeds

The following seeds are preregistered before execution:

`[7, 19, 43, 71, 101, 149, 211, 307]`

This gives 32 paired stochastic trajectories per controller: 4 disturbance families × 8 fixed seeds.

Each controller receives the exact same realized disturbance trajectory for a given family/seed pair.

## Controllers

1. Full frozen regulator.
2. Frozen `no_coupled_margin` regulator.

No additional controls are introduced in this experiment.

## Primary metrics

For each controller across the 32 paired trajectories:

- completion count
- viability count
- mean final progress
- mean resource remaining
- total failures
- repeated failures
- paired wins, ties, and losses under the frozen per-run score

The frozen AH-EXP-0005 score is computed independently for each seed over its four schedules, then aggregated across seeds.

## Primary hypothesis

If the coupled-margin mechanism is robust to stochastic variation, the full regulator should retain a positive aggregate advantage over the uncoupled regulator across the fixed seeds and should not rely on one isolated seed.

## Falsification criteria

The stochastic robustness claim is weakened or falsified if any of the following are true:

1. `no_coupled_margin` aggregate frozen score is greater than or equal to the full regulator aggregate score.
2. The full regulator is viable on fewer than 24 of 32 trajectories (75%).
3. The full regulator wins on fewer than 5 of the 8 preregistered seeds under the frozen four-schedule score.
4. The uncoupled controller completes at least 6 trajectories on which the full regulator does not complete.
5. Any post-outcome regulator, threshold, disturbance-amplitude, seed, scoring, or environment retuning is required to obtain the reported result.

## Interpretation constraint

Passing AH-EXP-0006 would support robustness of the coupled-margin effect under bounded stochastic perturbations in the controlled queue/service architecture. It would not establish a universal law, production safety, biological equivalence, autonomous-agent safety, or performance in real-world AI systems.
