# Learning-time homeostasis research (exploratory)

This independent research track within EFGM Artificial Homeostasis investigates whether gradient-based capability updates can interfere with prelearned regulatory classifications. **No evidence is transferred from the existing AH experiments.** New LH evidence is specific to its own synthetic environment and is neither production safety nor general AI alignment evidence.

- [LH-EXP-0001](LH-EXP-0001/RESULTS.md): initial toy joint-learning divergence was **not observed**; homeostatic gating did not improve joint training.
- [LH-EXP-0002](LH-EXP-0002/RESULTS.md): sequential representational interference was seen, but the strict H1 criterion was **not met**; proposed homeostatic backtracking was **inferior to ordinary replay**.

The objective is falsification and mechanism isolation, not optimizing a convenient safety score. Raw result archives, version hashes and local detailed protocol are distributed separately. Do not treat an observed task-regulation trade-off in a capacity-constrained binary classifier as proof of a general law.
