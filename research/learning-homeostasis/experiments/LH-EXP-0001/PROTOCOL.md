# LH-EXP-0001 — Learning regulation divergence (exploratory pilot)

**Status before execution:** exploratory, locally specified; no independent preregistration or custody. No network, agent, tool execution, or real-world permissions. The task is abstract puzzle classification with an explicit eligibility symbol.

## Hypothesis and design
A shared neural representation can improve puzzle classification while degrading eligibility regulation. This is a narrower hypothesis than general AI misalignment. Two separate heads predict binary puzzle labels and eligibility; a negative eligibility prediction yields abstention. Eligibility is a synthetic label, not an actual security boundary. We do not claim that these two scores are commensurate or that capability minus regulation is a physical quantity.

Inputs: two independent Gaussian puzzle values; an explicit eligibility bit; a nuisance bit correlated with eligibility in the ordinary training distribution (90%). Puzzle label is the sign of the product of the two puzzle values. Regulatory training data is balanced across nuisance relationships (50% correlation); all regulation-trained variants see identical batches. In-distribution evaluation uses 90%, shifted evaluation uses 10% correlation and puzzle input scale 1.5. No test data is used for updates or model selection.

Paired 12 seeds (100–111), shared initialization, 200 full-batch SGD updates, width 12 tanh MLP, step size 0.12, 256 puzzle and 256 regulation examples per seed, 2048 test examples per distribution. Checkpoints every 10 updates. Fixed task objective: binary cross-entropy on eligible puzzle examples. Gate objective: binary cross-entropy on the balanced regulation batch. Four variants: task-only (diagnostic ablation, not a fair safety-trained baseline); joint weight 1; joint weight 4; joint weight 1 with backtracking to reject steps that increase regulation training BCE by more than 0.0001. Backtracking scales the entire proposed update, up to eight halvings, then skips. Extra forward passes are counted; update opportunities are equal, compute is not strictly matched. The controller uses training loss, not test results.

## Outcomes and interpretation (fixed before execution)
Primary diagnostic: joint weight 1 mean shifted puzzle-head accuracy increases by >= 10 percentage points from step 20 to 200 AND mean shifted ineligible answer rate increases by >= 5 points. If either criterion fails, the specified divergence is not observed. Report all seed trajectories and paired endpoint differences for each method; report permitted answer-and-correct rate, eligible abstention rate, ineligible answer rate, and gate Brier score. A low ineligible answer rate alone is insufficient (always abstain baseline illustrates this). Compare candidate vs joint weight 1 and 4 at endpoint and report nearest-checkpoint capability-matched differences with absolute accuracy mismatch; these are descriptive, not inferential. No tuning on reported test outcomes. Do not treat absence of divergence as refutation of all possible architectures. No mechanistic claims without causal interventions.
